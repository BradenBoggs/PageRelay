# Referrals and service partnerships

Status: business-growth ideas discussed; not approved for MVP implementation.

This document owns customer referral incentives, attribution, partner referrals from external software companies, commissions, and payout safeguards.

## Purpose

SideWire may grow through two distinct programs:

- **Customer referrals:** existing users invite other companies and earn a product benefit when those companies become paying customers.
- **Service partnerships:** web-service providers with limited collaboration features refer their customers to SideWire and may receive a commission.

Keep these programs separate because their fraud risk, attribution, agreements, and payout administration differ.

## Customer referral direction

The simplest discussed offer is an earned subscription credit or free access after a defined number of referred organizations become and remain paying customers. Reward qualified paying conversions, not clicks, signups, invited teammates, or self-referrals.

Before implementation define qualification period, reward amount/cap, attribution window, refunds/chargebacks, duplicate accounts, abuse review, taxes, expiry, and what happens when the referring organization cancels.

Prefer account credits over cash payouts for an initial program because they are easier to administer and align the reward with continued product usage. This is a recommendation, not an approved offer.

## Service partnerships

Partner distribution is realistic only after SideWire proves retention and a clear complementary use case. A provider will need confidence that SideWire helps its users without competing for ownership of its core product or creating support/privacy problems.

Start with trackable referral links, co-marketing, or a simple recurring/revenue-share agreement. Do not build a full partner portal before a real signed partner requires it.

## Attribution and controls

Attribution must be server-owned, auditable, idempotent, and tied to qualified billing events. Users and partners cannot edit their own conversion state. Prevent self-referrals, repeated reward cycling, fake organizations, and payouts on refunded or disputed revenue.

Disclose referral relationships where required. Cash commissions need approved terms, tax handling, payout minimums, identity verification, and accounting workflow.

## Out of scope

Multi-level programs, open affiliate marketplaces, lifetime commissions without limits, rewards for raw leads, crypto payouts, and automated partner contracting are not approved.
